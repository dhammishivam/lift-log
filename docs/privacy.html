Lift Log privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Developer: KriShiv (Shivam Dhammi)

Lift Log is a workout tracker. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This policy covers the Lift Log Android app.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays on your phone: workouts, sets, routines, custom exercises, body measurements, progress photos and settings. Lift Log does not send this data to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account if you have device backup turned on. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

Files you import or export

When you import a file (for example a Hevy CSV export or a Lift Log backup), the app reads only the file you pick. When you export, the file is saved only where you choose. Nothing is uploaded.

Permissions

  • Notifications and alarms: to tell you when a rest timer ends while the app is in the background.
  • Vibration: to buzz when a rest timer ends.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick for progress pictures, through Android's photo picker. Copies are kept inside the app.
  • Internet: not used by the Play Store version for any of your data. It is declared for an optional AI coach that is not included in this version.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect, sell or share personal data. The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Deleting your data

All data lives on your phone. Delete individual items in the app, or uninstall the app (or clear its storage in Android settings) to remove everything.

Children

Lift Log is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
